INCIDENT INVESTIGATION
Incident ID: GH-2026-001
Organisation: Gulf Horizon Travel LLC

1. INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVE

To examine the potential impact of a simulated phishing
email targeting an employee's Microsoft 365 account.

The investigation focuses on:
- Whether the employee interacted with the email.
- Whether the suspicious link was accessed.
- Whether credentials may have been exposed.
- What evidence an SOC analyst would need to confirm
  or rule out account compromise.

NOTE:
This is a fictional cybersecurity training scenario.
No real employee, account or credentials are involved.


2. SIMULATED EMPLOYEE INTERACTION

Scenario:
An employee at Gulf Horizon Travel LLC receives
the suspicious Microsoft 365 verification email.

Simulated events:

09:12 - Phishing email received.
09:15 - Employee opens the email.
09:16 - Employee clicks the suspicious verification link.
09:17 - A fake Microsoft 365 login page is displayed.
09:18 - Employee enters fictional test credentials.
09:19 - The credentials are assumed exposed within
        this simulation.

Investigation status:
Simulated credential exposure.

Important:
These events are constructed for training purposes.
No actual link was visited and no credentials were
collected or transmitted.

3. EVIDENCE REQUIRED FOR VALIDATION

To investigate the simulated incident, an SOC analyst
would seek the following evidence:

1. Email Gateway Logs
   Purpose: Confirm email delivery and message details.

2. Web Proxy / DNS Logs
   Purpose: Determine whether the suspicious domain
   was accessed by the employee's device.

3. Endpoint Logs
   Purpose: Identify browser activity and related
   suspicious processes.

4. Microsoft 365 Sign-in Logs
   Purpose: Identify unusual login attempts,
   unfamiliar locations or suspicious sessions.

5. Authentication Logs
   Purpose: Identify failed or successful logins
   following potential credential exposure.

CURRENT FINDING:
Credential exposure is assumed within the simulation.
No actual endpoint, proxy or Microsoft 365 logs have
been collected to validate these events.

4. IMMEDIATE INCIDENT RESPONSE

If credential exposure were confirmed, the SOC team
would take the following actions:

1. Account Security
   - Reset the affected user's password.
   - Revoke active Microsoft 365 sessions.
   - Enforce multi-factor authentication.

2. Threat Containment
   - Block the confirmed malicious URL and domain.
   - Search email logs for other recipients.
   - Remove the phishing email from affected inboxes.

3. Investigation
   - Review sign-in and authentication logs.
   - Check for suspicious account activity.
   - Preserve relevant security evidence.

4. Recovery
   - Restore secure account access.
   - Monitor the account for further suspicious activity.
   - Document lessons learned.

NOTE:
These are proposed response actions, not actions
performed on a real account or network.
