CAMPAIGN COMPARISON
Incident: GH-2026-001
Reference: CPX Microsoft 365 QR-code phishing research
Publication date: 09 March 2026

CPX CAMPAIGN:
- QR codes embedded in email attachments.
- Fake Microsoft 365 authentication portals.
- Credential and session-token harvesting.
- Adversary-in-the-Middle techniques.
- Phishing-as-a-Service infrastructure.

GULF HORIZON SIMULATION:
- Microsoft 365 brand impersonation.
- Hyperlinked verification request.
- Urgent account suspension warning.
- Suspicious login URL.
- Potential credential exposure.

COMMON CHARACTERISTICS:
- Microsoft 365 impersonation.
- Credential-harvesting objective.
- Social engineering.

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCES:
- CPX campaign uses QR-code delivery.
- Gulf Horizon sample uses a direct hyperlink.
- AiTM activity has not been established in GH-2026-001.

ASSESSMENT:
The CPX campaign provides relevant threat context,
but there is insufficient evidence to attribute the
Gulf Horizon simulation to this campaign.
